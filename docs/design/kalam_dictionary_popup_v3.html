melancholy
/ˈmɛl.ən.ˌkɒl.i/
noun · adjective
Definitions
1.
A deep, persistent sadness; a gloomy state of mind, especially when habitual or prolonged.
"an air of melancholy hung over the deserted village"
2.
Having a feeling of melancholy; sad and pensive.
"she felt a melancholy longing for the past"
3.
Causing or expressing sadness; depressing.
"the melancholy of parting gave the scene a strange beauty"
4.
(historical) One of the four bodily humours, believed to cause depression when present in excess.
5.
A pensive mood; wistful thoughtfulness, not necessarily unpleasant.
"there is a gentle melancholy in his late compositions"

Idioms
in a melancholy mood
Experiencing a state of sadness and thoughtfulness, often without a clear reason.
a touch of melancholy
A mild, pleasantly nostalgic sadness; a gentle emotional heaviness that does not overwhelm.
born under a melancholy star
Destined, by nature or fate, to be prone to sadness; an old astrological expression for a gloomy temperament.
