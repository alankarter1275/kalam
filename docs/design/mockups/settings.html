Metadata: Books & Literature
Open Library
Internet Archive database. No key needed. Strong on older and public-domain titles.
Google Books
Broad coverage, good for recent and non-English books. Anonymous requests share a global quota.
ISFDB (Speculative Fiction)
The absolute gold standard for Sci-Fi, Fantasy, and Horror. Provides extremely detailed series, publisher, and awards data.
BookBrainz
An open, community-driven database. Highly structured data, continuously growing.
Metadata: Comics & Manga
Comic Vine
The industry standard database for Western comics. Fetches precise volume, issue, and creator credits.
AniList
Modern, developer-friendly database for Manga and Light Novels. Accurate high-quality cover artwork.
